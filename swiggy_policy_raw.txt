Swiggy Customer Care, Order & Refund Policy Guidelines

1. Order Cancellation Policy:
- Customers can cancel an order free of charge within 60 seconds of order placement.
- If an order is cancelled after 60 seconds, a cancellation fee of up to 100% of the total order value will be charged to compensate the merchant restaurant and delivery partners.
- If a restaurant or delivery partner cancels the order due to item non-availability, restaurant closure, or location unserviceability, no cancellation fee applies, and a 100% full refund is issued.

2. Refund Timelines and Methods:
- Refunds for cancelled orders or missing items are credited back to the original payment source (Credit/Debit Card, Netbanking, UPI) within 5 to 7 business days.
- For payments made through Swiggy Money / Swiggy Wallet, refunds are processed instantly within 2 hours.
- For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, refunds are credited to Swiggy Money or provided as coupons within 24 hours.

3. Quality, Damaged Items & Late Delivery Policy:
- If delivered food is damaged, spilled, spoiled, or has missing items, users must raise a ticket under 'Help with Orders' in the Swiggy app with photo evidence within 30 minutes of delivery.
- If an order is delivered significantly late beyond the estimated time of arrival (ETA) and the food arrives cold, a partial or full refund or reorder options may be provided based on support evaluation.
- Taste or personal food preference complaints are generally not eligible for full refunds, but feedback will be shared with the merchant.

4. Swiggy One Subscription Terms:
- Swiggy One members get unlimited free delivery on food orders above Rs. 199 from select restaurants.
- Members get free delivery on Swiggy Instamart orders above Rs. 99.
- Extra discounts up to 30% apply at participating restaurants under Swiggy One.
- Swiggy One membership fees are non-refundable once activated.

5. Swiggy Instamart & Genie Support:
- Instamart delivery issues, damaged grocery items, or missing products must be reported within 2 hours of delivery for instant wallet refund or replacement.
- Swiggy Genie pick-up and drop services carry an insurance coverage up to Rs. 2,500 for lost or damaged parcels during transit, provided prohibited items were not sent.

6. Customer Support Contact Channels:
- In-app Live Chat support is available 24/7 under the 'Help' section of the Swiggy app.
- Email support can be reached at support@swiggy.in for escalations.
